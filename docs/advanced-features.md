# Advanced Features Roadmap

This document tracks the design and implementation of advanced e-commerce features for AnyaBikini.

## Planned Modules
- Wishlist & Favorites
- Product Reviews & Ratings
- Size Guides & Fit Recommendations
- Product Variants (size, color, etc.)
- Inventory Management & Low-Stock Alerts
- Discount Codes, Gift Cards, Promotions
- Multi-currency & Internationalization
- High-Quality Images, Zoom, Video
- 360°/AR Product Views
- Personalized Recommendations
- Lookbooks & Shoppable Galleries
- PWA Support
- Analytics & Marketing Integrations
- Email Marketing & Abandoned Cart
- Loyalty & Referral Programs
- Social Sharing & Influencer Tools
- ERP/CRM/Fulfillment Integrations
- Shipping Provider Integrations
- Tax Calculation & Compliance
- Security & Compliance (PCI, GDPR, CCPA)
- CDN, Auto-scaling, Backups
- Live Chat, Chatbots, Support
- SEO, Blog, Content

## Implementation Notes
- Each feature will be a service/controller pair.
- All integrations use environment variables for secrets.
- Document API endpoints and business logic for each module.
